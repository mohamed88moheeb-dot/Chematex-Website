How we run trips

A route goes in the catalogue after we've walked it, not before.

Meridian started in 2011 with one route and two guides. There are twenty-six routes now and thirty-one guides, and the rule has not changed: somebody who works here has done the whole thing, in the season it will be sold in, before a place goes on sale.

Plotting

A route usually starts with a guide who already lives somewhere and knows a way through it that isn't the way everyone else goes. The Wadi Rum traverse exists because Ahmad wanted people to come into Petra over the ridge rather than through the ticket gate.

From there it takes about eighteen months. We walk it, we walk it again in worse weather, we find out which guesthouse actually has hot water in April, and we build the day-by-day around what the ground allows rather than what reads well.

Pricing

Every route page lists what the price covers and what it doesn't, with amounts where we know them. Park fees, permits, hut bookings and transfers are always in. Flights never are, because you can nearly always book them better than we can.

Prices move with the season. The middle of a route's window costs more because that is when it runs best — we would rather say so than pretend every date is equal.

Guides

Thirty-one guides across six regions, all from the region they work in, all on a retainer through the off-season. That last part is the expensive one and it is the reason the same people come back year after year.

Every lead guide holds a current wilderness first-aid certificate. On alpine routes graded four or five, the lead is IFMGA-qualified or the national equivalent, and there is a second guide on the ground.

Deposits, refunds and weather

A 20% deposit holds a place and is fully refundable for fourteen days. The balance falls due sixty days before departure. Cancel after that and we refund what we can recover from the lodges and permit offices, which on most routes is more than half.

If we cancel — for conditions, for a guide's illness, for anything at all — you get the whole amount back or a place on another date, whichever you prefer. We have never run a trip because it was already paid for, and we rerouted eleven trips last year rather than push on.

Travel insurance covering medical evacuation is required on every route graded three and above. We will ask to see it.
