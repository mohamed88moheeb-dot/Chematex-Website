About Chematex

Over 30 years of chemical formulation in Cairo.

Chematex is a Cairo-based chemical manufacturer and supplier. Our strength lies in combining technical knowledge with reliable supply: formulation experience, quality control and a practical understanding of what our clients run every day.

How we grew

Founded on textile auxiliaries. Grown into six lines.

The company was established with a strong focus on textile auxiliaries and has since expanded into detergents and cleaners, personal care, sanitizers and disinfectants, RO water treatment chemicals and general raw materials.

    Inside the plant

    From brief to delivery, under one roof.

    Every Chematex product follows the same route through our New Cairo plant.

    1. 01R&D lab
    2. 02Water treatment
    3. 03Blending
    4. 04Packed & shipped
    01R&D and formulation

    It starts in our lab.

    We formulate something new, match your textile sample, tailor a solution to your brief, or improve a sample you give us.

    02Water treatment

    RO water, the base.

    Softening columns and a reverse-osmosis unit treat the process water before it goes into any formulation, so hard-water salts never reach the batch.

    03Blending

    Formulated in stainless steel.

    Agitated stainless-steel tanks blend each product to its recipe, from large production batches to smaller runs.

    Then it's labelled, stored and sent to your site.
